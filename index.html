Transparency

Where every number comes from

Poles are counted as starts from grid slot 1 (qualifying data is only published from 2003). Fastest laps are published from 2004 onwards. Privateer Ferrari entries of the 1950s are included, as in the source database. Race-day weather is ERA5 reanalysis on a ~25 km grid around the circuit, not a trackside measurement.
